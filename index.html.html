Ethical Framework & Scientific Scope

1. Probabilistic Uncertainty Disclosure

Sub-seasonal skill at block scale has inherent limits past 14 days. Every output carries an explicit confidence class (High, Medium, Low) and uncertainty bounds are never hidden from officers or farmers.

2. High-Impact Gating & Approval

Irreversible agricultural advice (e.g. "Delay nursery sowing for 7 days") is strictly gated on Medium/High confidence and requires Extension Officer digital sign-off before mass SMS/WhatsApp broadcast.

3. Data Privacy & Template i18n

MonsoonSight operates on minimal farmer contact data with explicit opt-out compliance. All regional advice stems from agronomist-reviewed template slots, strictly avoiding free-form generative LLM hallucinations.
